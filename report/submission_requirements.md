## Blocking inputs for SP-08.2

Format-specific report drafting is blocked until report language, an approved template or explicit template-neutral drafting authorization, an official or explicitly approved working title, student and supervisor identity fields, citation style, and a submission deadline or accepted interim schedule are confirmed. Product imagery is additionally blocked until a preserved file and reproduction-permission decision exist. Content planning may proceed while these gates remain open.

## Non-blocking inputs for early drafting

Evidence selection, section planning, claim qualification, table/figure placement planning, bibliography metadata review, and appendix mapping may proceed in a template-neutral form. Page range, reference count, portal, naming, presentation timing, archive/tag, checksum, and signature rules do not block this architecture stage, but their owners and later blocking stages remain recorded.

## Human review checklist

- Students obtain and preserve current university or department instructions.
- Supervisor confirms title, scope, report language, identity fields, citation style, and drafting authorization.
- Students confirm deadline, portal, file naming, confidentiality, and final sign-off workflow.
- Project owner supplies any original product capture and provenance; rights holder/supervisor decide reproduction permission.
- Department confirms presentation, question-and-answer, demonstration, archive, checksum, and signature requirements.
- Human reviewers resolve conflicts explicitly; no missing value is inferred from an example report or project plan.
